About this blog

Built out of emotions that all lost people go through. Frustration, confusion, urge to start *something* (not knowing what that should or would be), all that jazz. It's basically a diary, and it exists for reasons why people use diaries. The difference is that it's open to everyone. You'll get to know me. And for me, you. Hi.

The blog will be in one of three languages (English, Japanese or Korean) depending on which headspace I am in at the time of writing. The blog is mostly written in plain HTML for a specific reason, scroll down for more. Stuff I write should be translatable, so if you wish to read, please do so. Better yet, learn the language yourself!
